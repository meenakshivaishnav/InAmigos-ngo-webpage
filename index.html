About Us

Working together to build a more compassionate, inclusive and sustainable society.

Who We Are

Founded in 2020, InAmigos Foundation is a Section 8 registered non-profit organization headquartered in Bilaspur, Chhattisgarh. The organization believes that meaningful change begins when passionate individuals come together with a common purpose.

Through volunteer-driven initiatives, awareness campaigns and community projects, the foundation is actively working across multiple states of India to improve lives and promote sustainable development.

Our Mission

To empower communities through education, social welfare, environmental sustainability and youth participation.

Our Vision

To create an inclusive society where every individual has the opportunity to learn, grow and contribute towards nation building.

Our Flagship Projects

Every initiative focuses on solving real-world problems while encouraging active community participation.

Project SEVA

Focuses on providing food, clothing and other essential resources to underprivileged communities while promoting compassion and volunteerism.

Project BACHPANSHALA

Dedicated to improving access to education through learning support, digital literacy, mentoring and skill development for children.

Project JEEV

An initiative dedicated to the rescue, care and welfare of stray animals by promoting compassion and responsible community participation.

Project UDAAN

Empowers women through awareness programmes, skill development, leadership opportunities and financial independence initiatives.

Project PRAKRITI

Promotes environmental sustainability through plantation drives, cleanliness campaigns and environmental awareness activities.

Project VIKAS

Equips students and young professionals with internships, practical exposure and industry-relevant skills to enhance their careers.
